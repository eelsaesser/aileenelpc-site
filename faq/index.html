Frequently Asked Questions

How long are appointments?

Appointments are generally 55 minutes long, however can be adjusted if necessary and previously discussed.

Do you accept insurance?

I am an Out-of-Network provider, which means I do not take insurance, but I do provide Superbills after sessions, which you can submit to your insurance company for reimbursement.

What is your cancellation policy?

I have a 24 hour cancellation policy.

This is necessary because a time commitment is made to you and is held exclusively for you. Cancellations within 24 hours will be charged the full fee.

What is your fee?

My fee is $185 per 55 minute appointment.

How do telehealth appointments work?

I use a HIPAA compliant platform, Simple Practice, which will send you a link by email or text for our appointments. It is very easy to use.
